# Common AI-Slop Vocabulary and Openers

These are warning signals, not absolute bans. A word can be correct in its
literal or technical meaning. Review it when it is vague, ornamental,
interchangeable with a simpler term, or repeated across nearby drafts. Prefer
concrete language or restructure the sentence when the wording adds no meaning.

## Review Principle

Do not infer authorship from one word, opener, punctuation mark, or model-era
list. Look for clusters and function: vague vocabulary, repeated structures,
unsupported certainty, generic framing, and prose that could be detached from
the author's actual material. Replace the reasoning or sentence relationship
before swapping synonyms.
